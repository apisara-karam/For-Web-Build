Strategic thesis · 05

Fix the operating model first.

The primary gaps are ownership, definitions and data quality—not a shortage of tooling.

01 / Ownership

Name accountable domain owners and operational stewards. Give them decision rights and escalation paths.

02 / Definitions

Agree critical data elements, glossary terms and lineage before scaling reporting or AI.

03 / Quality

Set rules, thresholds, issue workflows and evidence of remediation for priority data products.

Tools can support these controls; they cannot establish accountability or approve business meaning.

Operating model · 11

Govern the outcomes.

Decision rights

Executive sponsor funds and resolves conflicts; governance council approves policy; domain owners sign off definitions; stewards run controls; security/privacy partners review obligations.

Risks & responses

Unclear ownership → signed RACI. Adoption fatigue → small pilot and training. Poor source quality → issue backlog. Scope/cost growth → stage gates and consumption alerts.

Evidence of success

Track % critical elements with owners and definitions; % passing quality rules; median issue resolution time; % priority sources cataloged; policy exceptions and annual re-score.

Set baselines and numeric thresholds with business owners during the pilot; do not confuse tool adoption counts with mature governance outcomes.
